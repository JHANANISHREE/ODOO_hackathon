Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Brightloop architecture and subscription workflows.

What guarantees idempotent recurring billing?
Every invoice generation verifies period boundaries against existing invoices. Even if an automated billing batch runs multiple times, a unique database constraint prevents duplicate records from ever being created.
How does multi-variant product pricing calculate?
Each product variant inherits the base sales price and adds attribute-specific extra pricing. Taxes and applicable percentage or fixed discounts are computed in real time.
Can customers pause or resume their subscriptions?
Yes. Customers can pause subscriptions directly from the customer portal if the plan allows pausing. While paused, automated billing cycles are skipped until resumed.
Are partial payments supported?
Yes. Multiple payments (via card, bank transfer, or UPI) can be applied against an invoice until the balance reaches zero, at which point the invoice status transitions to Paid.
